REPUBLIC OF TALOS
A game by Miguel Guerrero | https://miguelguerrero.eu

Online: https://miguelguerrero.eu/talos-game/

1. Extract the play ZIP.
2. Open PLAY.html in Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.
3. Select Enter the republic and read the three-part prologue.
4. Read one incident, prepare if useful, and make one decision.

Three separate futures. 36 decisions. About two hours, at your pace.
The game, images, films, original lofi music and sound effects work offline.
Sound starts quietly when you enter. Use the header to mute or Menu > Music & sound for volume and SFX.

Progress saves automatically in this browser. Menu offers export/import for portable saves.
Existing saves from the prior edition remain compatible.

The research is real; Talos and its future incidents are fictional.
ERA: Capability, Deployment, Effect: https://miguelguerrero.eu/agencytransfer/
Full research and limits are in the game's Research & credits panel.

EDITION 4
Every response has a human aftermath. Expand the wider impact or research when useful.
The short films can be paused, skipped or replayed; they never advance a decision.
Use Menu > Reduce ambient movement for still presentations.
