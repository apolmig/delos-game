PLAY OFFLINE:
1. Extract the play ZIP.
2. Open PLAY.html in a modern browser and let the large file load.
3. Enter the republic and read the three-part prologue.
4. Read one incident, prepare if useful, and make one decision.

Three possible futures. 36 decisions. About two hours at your pace.
This is a design estimate; watching all optional explainers adds 25 minutes.

Each decision has a different 42-second film explaining the human stakes and response paths. Use the film button before deciding or after seeing the result. Films are optional and never advance the game. A transcript is always available.

The prologue and 2027/2028 briefings explain the AI, its permissions, the uncertainty and European regulatory context. Research and legal details are optional, so the main turn stays simple.

Images, films, original quiet music and sound effects work offline.
Use the header to mute, or Menu > Music & sound for volume and SFX.
Use Menu > Reduce ambient movement for still presentations.

Progress saves automatically in this browser. Export/import saves through the menu to move them. Prior saves remain compatible.

The research is real; Talos and its incidents are fictional.
