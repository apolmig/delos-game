PLAY OFFLINE:
1. From Drive, download both Republic_of_Delos_Play.zip and Republic_of_Delos_Decision_Films.zip. Extract both into the same folder: assets should sit beside PLAY.html.
2. Open PLAY.html in a modern browser. Keep the assets folder beside it.
3. Enter the republic and listen to or read the three-part prologue.
4. Read one incident, prepare if useful, and make one decision.

Three possible futures. 36 decisions. About two hours at your pace.
This is a design estimate; watching all optional explainers adds 25 minutes.

For a single-file offline download, use https://miguelguerrero.eu/delos-game/PLAY.html instead. That larger file embeds every film and does not need an assets folder.

Editing the source: extract the film ZIP inside the source archive's civic-mandate folder before running the build. The film library serves both the play and source packages.

Each decision has a different narrated 42-second film explaining who is affected and what each response could change. Use the film button before deciding or after seeing the result. Films are optional and never advance the game. A transcript is always available.

The prologue and 2027/2028 briefings explain the AI, its permissions, what remains uncertain and the relevant European rules. Research and legal details are optional, so each decision stays simple.

All 36 films and the three prologue chapters have natural English voice narration. Listen/Pause controls and transcripts keep reading optional.
Images, narration, films, original quiet music and sound effects work offline.
Use the header to mute all sound. Menu > Music & sound adjusts background music and cues; speech remains clear at its own level.
Use Menu > Reduce ambient movement for still presentations.

Progress saves automatically in this browser. Export/import saves through the menu to move them. Prior saves remain compatible.

The research is real; Delos and its incidents are fictional.
