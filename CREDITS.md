# Republic of Talos / credits

Game authored by [Miguel Guerrero](https://miguelguerrero.eu).

[ERA: Capability, Deployment, Effect](https://miguelguerrero.eu/agencytransfer/).

- Original fictional world and character sculptures created locally with Blender 5.2.2 LTS. Editable source is included.
- Three.js 0.180.0, MIT licence: assets/THREE-LICENSE.txt.
- Barlow font, SIL Open Font License: assets/FONT-LICENSE.txt.
- Eight moving scenes reuse existing Talos AI-generated footage, originally produced with Seedance 2.0 Fast through ElevenLabs Creative Studio. They depict fictional people and situations. Film provenance is in assets/cinema/manifest.json.
- Eighteen incident illustrations reuse the earlier Talos image collection; assets/scenes/manifest.json records their provenance. Nine additional human-impact images were made with built-in imagegen for edition 4; prompts, filenames and hashes are in assets/scenes/outcomes-manifest.json. All 27 are fictional dramatizations.
- Thirty-six distinct 42-second decision explainers were composed and rendered with HyperFrames. They combine existing moving scenes and illustrations with original captions, animated diagrams, option comparisons and quiet music. These are new edits, not 36 newly filmed incidents. Scripts, timings, option identities and source IDs are in assets/explainers/manifest.json. The separate private Talos production archive includes editable compositions and rebuild instructions.
- Game music: Night Session, an original 72 BPM, 160-second lofi composition created with electric-piano synthesis, bass, brushed percussion and room echoes. No third-party samples or recordings; reproducible with compose-score.py and FFmpeg.
- Explainer music: a quiet original procedural instrumental bed, documented in the production archive.
- Sound effects: original short procedural cues generated locally with Web Audio.
- Research references, scope and limitations are in RESEARCH.md and the game. The general bibliography cites public research originals. Citations imply no endorsement by researchers, providers, Talos or course contributors.

The game contains no official Civilization assets, branding, code or affiliation. Progress remains local. The web edition loads assets from its own host. The standalone PLAY.html needs no network; only explicitly opened research links visit external sites. No private course slides, recordings, participant details or credentials are distributed.

- English narration: synthetic voice generated with ElevenLabs, George, Eleven v4. Scene-aligned narration is mixed over a quietly carved original score.
- Social card: original AI-generated cinematic artwork for this game.
