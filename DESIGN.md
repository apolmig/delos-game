# Republic of Talos / edition 3

Author: Miguel Guerrero, https://miguelguerrero.eu

The design puts one incident and one binding decision at the centre. Three visible indicators are services, rights and independence, alongside funds. Trust and council support remain in the rules and can be inspected in Republic status; alerts surface when attention is needed. One optional preparation replaces two cabinet actions in the default view. Case findings cost no preparation or funds, encouraging evidence use.

A three-part prologue establishes the middle-power country, the player's limited democratic authority, the separate futures and the play loop. Detailed strategy tools remain available through the menu. The original 13-district Blender world and eight characters remain.

Every incident has an explicit research mapping in src/research.js. The mapping separates observed findings from authored policy tradeoffs. The full source library gives limitations. Numerical effects are fictional balancing choices. No research effect size becomes a vote multiplier.

The original bundled lofi score starts at 8% after the player enters the game with sound selected. Music softens during panels and pauses when the tab is hidden. A single mute switch controls all sound, and settings allow volume adjustment or disabling SFX. No external player is created.

Save schema version 1 is retained for compatibility. Missing simple-view preferences migrate to the simpler interface; explicit preferences are preserved. Import never fetches or executes content from the save.

Each decision has a scene and an illustrated human consequence mapped to the stable option route. Story text does not change scores, thresholds or balance. The three recurring fictional people receive a prologue, an eighteen-month transition and an outcome-dependent epilogue.
